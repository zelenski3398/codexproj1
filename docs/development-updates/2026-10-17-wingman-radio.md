The existing Faith, Hope and Charity mission now offers **T — Request wingman status** while piloting Faith, without pausing or changing aircraft input/camera/AI orders. The binding lives in Input Map; the briefing and HUD reflect its current label. `WingmanRadio` queues Faith's request and each responsive survivor, builds replies at playback time, applies cooldown/pending guards, and skips/cancels dead speakers. Both lost produces a short delayed No response, without repeated loss announcements.

The three new MP3s are fixed dialogue recordings. Local automatic recognition identifies **RAF Pilot 2 as Faith**, **Walke Talkie Commander Dude as Hope**, and **RAF Pilot voice as Charity**; recognition of the cropped outputs independently confirms all three names. Faith's full take describes one bomber/three escorts and an attack, Hope's says he will follow Charity, and Charity's says he will distract fighters. They cannot truthfully serve as universal live status reports. The implementation therefore uses their naturally paused **Control, this is <pilot>** openings and detailed truthful subtitles. Full takes are retained separately for future suitable cues, but no AI tactics are changed to justify a fixed line. No new speech is synthesized. Full spoken request/condition lines remain missing and are enumerated in the guide.

`WingmanSpotter` supplies independent, bounded sighting memory for each wingman: 3,000 m range, 220-degree FOV, terrain/active-building ray obstruction and 0.35 s scans. Reports refresh perception before assembly, never exposing future waves, hidden current coordinates or enemy HP. Recent contacts use immutable last-known positions for at most 15 seconds and are explicitly labelled lost visual. Clock bearings use Faith's current heading, with compass fallback when unreliable/stationary. Damage tiers and engine/control/fire/fuel information come from existing component/health state; activity comes from current AI state. Geometrically observed threats and bomber/fort approaches outrank less useful details.

The shared `MissionRadioQueue` now supports speaker-labelled deferred reports, validity predicates, reply spacing, cancellation and urgent commander preemption while retaining the original commander API. Attack-run and loss warnings interrupt routine requests; results retain full priority. Existing Master routing, temporary Aircraft/Guns ducking and native-stream/web-sample playback are reused. Status audio is a short identifier; detailed text stays visible for a configurable reading window. Retry/menu exit clears the queue, request cooldown/IDs and perception; voice assignments remain stable.

Preparation uses normalization and fades, preserving the supplied radio treatment instead of stacking a second heavy filter/static layer. Source hashes, filenames and crop bounds accompany six Ogg assets (full/identification for each pilot). The new preparation script, two runtime components, shared queue/configuration/mission/UI/input edits, targeted tests and captures are documented in the [radio guide](../wingman-radio.md), together with parameter defaults, missing recordings and native screenshots. The previous mission guide and root README link to it. Publishing CI runs the new suite.
