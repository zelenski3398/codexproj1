The new **Faith, Hope and Charity — Harbour Interception** menu entry starts the existing continuous Malta mission at Ħal Far with Faith, a player Sea Gladiator, engine stopped. Two friendly Sea Gladiators wait behind its departure corridor and take off under existing force-driven controls after Faith sustains six seconds above 65 m AGL. Their success does not gate activation. `FriendlyGladiator` and `HarbourCombatPilot` reuse the existing Sea Gladiator physics, guns, component damage and effects. Hope/Charity have individual HP, target assignments, return-to-formation behaviour and persistent losses. Both can be lost without ending the mission.

`HarbourInterception` and reusable `AircraftWaveRoster` create exactly **4, 4 and 3** enemies: three SM.79 bombers, six normal CR.42 escorts and two final-wave ace CR.42s. Existing procedural aircraft models and audio are reused. Aces improve configurable aim, dispersion, navigation prediction and target distribution, preserving normal weapon damage, speed, firing rate and swept collision. Escorts protect their bomber and remain alive after its loss. Explicit withdrawal and contact timeouts prevent a vanished bomber wreck or unreachable escort from stalling the mission, without inventing kills.

Whole-fort HP did not previously exist. Two mission-local `FortObjective` receivers default to 1,000 HP. Their actual footprints derive from the existing St Elmo peninsula transform and St Angelo Cottonera source OSM `way/54896162`, using the existing EPSG:32633 world origin. Interior aim points use live ground/structure elevation. No city geometry or coordinates change. The existing `BomberPilot` flies toward a ballistic release point; physical `MissionBomb` objects inherit velocity, fall under gravity and damage forts through actual impact blasts. A release, player loss or fort destruction fails once. Success requires three intercepted bombers, resolved escorts and two surviving forts. Retry clears the mission, friends, enemies, ordnance, effects and radio state.

The two supplied commander MP3s were prepared with the existing radio-processing function as `assets/audio/harbour/intro.ogg` and `congratulations.ogg`. Sidecars retain source hashes and processing metadata; no speech is synthesized. `MissionRadioQueue` provides serial non-spatial playback, one-shot identifiers, 4 dB temporary engine/weapon ducking, result priority and teardown. The recordings use explicitly labelled summary captions because no verified transcript was supplied. Other cues are subtitle-only with optional recording slots. The original `MissionRadio` missing-asset behaviour is unchanged.

Small integration edits add menu/start/retry/end-panel routing, Faith-only boarding eligibility, opt-in bomb-to-fort blast damage, projectile shooter-ID snapshots and last-hit attribution. The new fort JSON is explicitly included in Web export, and four new suites run in publishing CI. [The mission guide](../harbour-interception.md) contains controls, architecture/state diagrams, component references, numeric tuning, geographic derivation, aircraft/audio limitations and three inspected native previews.
