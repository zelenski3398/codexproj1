# 21 November 2026 — Wartime villages and churches

The [scenery source commit](https://github.com/zelenski3398/codexproj1/commit/702eeacffa73768b7b42620b78f61d01b7f7d7ab) adds 22 wartime districts, 53 terrain-seated church and chapel exteriors, traced village streets, residential plots, farmyards and provisional coastal military compounds. The existing Mosta and Mdina models are part of the same world. The terrain, chart coordinates, airfields and mission starts still use the shared Malta geography.

The native scenery checks loaded 7,911 buildings in 1,244 geometry batches and skipped five plots for steep or missing ground. The capture script produced 48 images across the new and existing areas. Software rendering in the Qormi capture took 233 ms/frame at 55 m and 178 ms/frame at 600 m, while other checks were running. These measurements are specific to the test runner. Hardware performance and a full browser playthrough remain unverified.

The map edition, one-point crop residuals and some peripheral chapel identifications remain open. The Pembroke and St Julian’s area uses a provisional layout from heritage references because the coastal sheet could not be identified among the supplied screenshots.
