# 25 November 2026 — Survey-based Malta elevation and ground refit

The mainland uses additional terrain survey samples to resolve the slopes and ridges in the supplied reference views. The same corrected mesh feeds visual LODs and collision. The refit includes the three runways, roads and access links, city meshes, rural plots, wall collision, tree roots and terrain-relative placement. The work is on the separate `feature/malta-elevation` branch for review. [Calibration, coordinates and rebuild](../malta-elevation.md) · [Source commit](https://github.com/zelenski3398/codexproj1/commit/23a7e5f6712b08b4247531e98c2d72328c357ac0)

The north-up and oblique review was compared visually against both screenshots. The offline checks passed 27 countryside tests, 21 Cottonera landscape tests and 15 Cottonera source assertions. Refit soil skin vertices followed the exported ground to within 0.27 mm in the countryside check. The field slope filter omitted 1,000 previously baked plots and 40 short rubble-wall sections. The remaining placements retain their plan coordinates. [Review image](validation/malta-elevation-review.png)

The corrected terrain passed 18 targeted Godot suites. The 38 terrain checks include surveyed control-point collision heights, continuity across a legacy triangle edge, and runway support at all three airfields. The flight fixture taxied and took off at Ta’ Qali, then landed and braked at Ħal Far. Mission checks covered recovery, the harbour defence, Fort St Elmo, the tutorial and the duel authority. After preserving all 880 Valletta footprints with road-patch ground sampling, the Valletta, countryside, harbour and Fort St Elmo suites passed again. The release web export built locally.

A focused Chromium smoke test of the local release passed 22 checks. It used the loading cinematic’s Skip button and skipped the native poster through the fixture. It exercised Luqa departure, real WebGL 2 terrain and settlement loading, collision presence and the F4 heatmap round trip. Full browser flights and a manual audit of every individual building or crossing were outside the observed coverage.
