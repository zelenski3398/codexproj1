# Wartime villages of Malta

Use the chart in **Malta Free Roam** to explore the historic village cores. The same scenery appears in missions set in the continuous Malta world. The reconstruction depicts standing architecture around the beginning of 1942, before the documented spring raids. Settlement outlines and street branches were traced from the supplied screenshots. Most individual lots and façades are inferred within the hatched areas.

## Alignment and open questions

- **Attard, Lija, Balzan and Birkirkara.** The shared crop fits at 3.3934 m/pixel with 0.245° rotation. Residuals for Lija, Balzan, Birkirkara and Attard are 17.67, 23.35, 20.19 and 25.75 m. Attard’s main trace comes from its dedicated crop. Each church uses its independently projected footprint.
- **Żurrieq, Nigret and Bubaqra.** The shared crop fits at 2.2818 m/pixel and −3.254°. Residuals are 28.66, 28.58 and 25.48 m. The separate Safi crop shows the Żurrieq fork. The Kirkop crop overlaps Safi and shows part of Gudja.
- **Two-control registrations.** Kirkop–Safi, Safi–Żurrieq and Rabat–Mdina fit both controls exactly by construction. Independent residuals remain unmeasured. The Rabat tie to the existing Mdina reference has substantial bearing uncertainty.
- **Adjustable one-point registrations.** Attard’s dedicated crop, Għargħur, Qormi, Mqabba, Naxxar, Qrendi, Siġġiewi and Żebbuġ use 2.85 m/pixel north-up about the main church marker. Independent controls would be needed to quantify the error. The road mask replaces local road paint. New road ends within 76 m of an existing path can connect at clear approaches. Some branches terminate at the traced boundary.
- **Other map labels.** San Anton has its Pilar chapel. The palace, formal gardens, Wignacourt Aqueduct, reservoirs, pumping stations, Victoria Lines and signal station, quarry details, Rohan Arch and the Dominican Monastery have no new bespoke geometry. Terrain and existing airfields remain in place. The exact relationship of the Salvatore, Loreto, S. Nicola, S. Agata, S. Giovanni, Tal-Grazzja, S. Maria and other peripheral chapel markings to surviving footprints requires confirmation. The freestanding historical photographs lack captions that establish the subject and date.
- **North coast.** The referenced coastal crop could not be identified in the supplied screenshot set. The barrack layouts are adjustable sketches. The clock tower and Australia Hall use their reported sites with estimated exteriors. Period sources document St Andrew’s Christ Church, but its precise site and wartime exterior are unresolved. St Julian’s Lapsi Church and the Immaculate Conception at Spinola are represented. The Balluta Carmelite church’s 1942 phase still needs a verified footprint and façade. Pembroke’s late-war chapel and modern Resurrection of Christ parish fall outside the baseline. - **Period exteriors.** Historic tower heights, door details and roof forms remain estimates where the sources are incomplete. Mqabba is shown before the loss of its dome and transept. The Qrendi Local Council documents 1942 damage to both St Matthew buildings. Attard has a central octagonal drum, smaller transept domes and a single belfry. Safi has a single belfry, clock and dome. ## Terrain, materials and damage

Church and house foundations sample the terrain collider. The street and square surfaces use an overlay on the original terrain material. A mask and spatial lookup clear overlapping paint, ground decoration, trees and rural wall collisions where replacement scenery is present. The terrain mesh and all three runway locations are retained. Recovery pickups sample new street segments. The houses reuse Mosta’s flat roofs, parapets, doors, windows, roof rooms, balconies and limestone variants. Selected church-square and farm buildings use shared structural damage. The church bodies and most houses use simplified static collision.

Districts build across physics frames. Shared geometry uses 128 m MultiMesh chunks. New village shells cull at 4.2 km and façade detail culls at 330 m. Church masses and trim merge into material surfaces with shorter range for close detail.

## Rebuilding and checks

- Adjust pixel controls and outlines in [traces.json](../assets/malta/villages/traces.json). Adjust source anchors and model details in [church_footprints.json](../assets/malta/villages/church_footprints.json). Inspect projected metre coordinates and scene records in [layout.json](../assets/malta/villages/layout.json). OpenStreetMap footprint coordinates are attributed to its contributors under ODbL 1.0. [OpenStreetMap attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
- Run `python3 tools/generate_wartime_villages.py` with NumPy, Pillow, pyproj and Shapely to produce the deterministic geometry records and masks.
- Run `godot --headless --fixed-fps 120 --script tests/wartime_villages_checks.gd` to check district counts, church pivots, foundation seating, building batches, recovery-road samples and airfield spawns.
- Set `WARTIME_CAPTURE_DIR` and run `godot --resolution 700x440 --script tests/wartime_villages_capture.gd` with a graphics renderer. Pass the capture and output folders to `python3 tools/compose_wartime_village_atlas.py`.

The native reconstruction ran with 53 registered exteriors and 7,911 instantiated lots. Five plots were skipped by terrain checks. The export packaged both the new registry and terrain texture. Software-rendered captures cover all 24 areas. The browser test reached the airborne St Elmo objective and accepted a bomb release, then timed out while waiting for impact. A sustained browser flight and hardware frame-rate measurement are still pending. [Development journal](development-updates/2026-11-21-wartime-villages.md)
