# Under Dive-Bomber Attack

Select **Missions → Under Dive-Bomber Attack → Defend Ħal Luqa**. Start beside Luqa's existing two guns. **E** mounts/dismounts; **arrows** aim, **Ctrl** fires (browser also supports F), **Space** gives fine aiming, **W/S** adjusts airburst distance. On foot use WASD and arrow turning; E also operates the existing truck. **M** opens the map, **Escape** pauses, **R** fully retries. This dedicated mission has no aircraft fallback, base-health objective or interception deadline.

Wave counts are exactly **1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 1 ace**. Nothing spawns until the first mount, followed by four seconds preparation. All aircraft in a wave spawn together, safely spaced. Individual runs are staggered by six seconds. Actual destruction/crash events clear the wave, then outstanding bombs/hostile rounds drain, followed by a configurable ten-second interval. Health is never repaired between waves. Live enemy projectile pools are retained in the mission when a shooter dies, so its already-fired rounds continue sweeping until impact/expiry. The shared aircraft controller keeps its previous destruction behaviour in other modes. Victory waits for all ten losses and all remaining hostile ordnance, with a living player and at least one local gun; operator death or both local guns lost fails once.

`DiveDefenceMission` owns progression/results and finite bombs. `DiveAttackPilot` reuses `EnemyPilot`'s terrain scans, bank/throttle navigation, damage compensation and the aircraft's existing force-driven flight. It commands an approach, physical dive, ballistic release, recovery and another run. Each aircraft has one bomb; later runs use real swept gunfire. The final ace has ordinary Stuka physics and 100 HP, with better configurable aim and shorter recovery. There are no guaranteed hits or direct scripted injuries.

`GroundPilot` reuses `AircraftHealth` independently of aircraft/gun HP. The invisible mounted pilot keeps a live capsule at the gunner position. Enemy bullets include its collision layer. Bomb damage snapshots both operator and guns before any ejection, applies operator injury first, and resolves outcomes after same-tick damage. Each bomb's impact latch prevents duplicate application. Three cover probes attenuate exposed blast paths; gun/sandbag cover reduces rather than eliminates damage. Terrain/buildings can block it. The truck follows the driver capsule and offers partial blast protection. Other modes keep their existing gun-only bomb rules and disabled ground-pilot combat.

Balance is editable in `mission_definitions/dive_defence.tres` / `DiveDefenceDefinition`: 1,600 m spawn distance, 620 m initial terrain clearance, 260 m formation spacing, four-second first countdown, ten-second intervals, six-second attack staggering; normal/ace bomb error 18/7 m and strafe aim error 0.9/0.25°, recovery 14/9 s. Normal/ace strafing run-in distances are 1,700/1,800 m with 8/5° entry alignment: the ace takes a cleaner firing approach through ordinary controls. Bomb radius 35 m, maximum gun damage 180 and operator damage 130, both distance-dependent with cover. Existing AA ballistics/damage, forward-gun ballistics, crash smoke and explosions remain in use.

Bomb impacts reuse the existing CPU destruction burst. The project has no dedicated bomb-explosion recording; bomb impacts reuse the low-pitched cannon effect, with a four-voice cap; this is illustrative, not a historical recording. No new voice or paid asset was introduced.

Tests: `dive_defence_checks.gd` covers menu, mount/countdown, empty guns, swept operator hits, independent persistent HP, live gun/truck travel, destruction/ejection, same-bomb lethal outcomes, all six exact batches, ace tuning, outstanding ordnance, failure/victory latches and retry. `dive_attack_checks.gd` observes an uninterrupted real physics attack/recovery/strafe cycle; its bomb injuries are disabled only in that observation fixture. Separate tests use normal blast damage. Full manual six-wave combat balance, subjective sound mix and performance on the user's hardware require playtesting.

Validation: **47/47 mission-state checks**, **6/6 real attack-cycle checks**, **611/611 assertions across 14 existing regression groups**, and **10/10 release-browser mission checks** passed. The observed first bomb impacted about **28.3 m** from a gun; the aircraft completed its recovery and returned for strafing, with minimum observed terrain clearance **270.6 m**. These measurements describe one deterministic normal-pilot fixture, not a guarantee for every damaged airframe or a subjective six-wave balance assessment.
