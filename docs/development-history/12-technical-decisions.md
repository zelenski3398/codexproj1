## Architectural consequences to watch

Direct references make the prototype easy to follow but couple mission, HUD and interaction state. `AACommandStation` inherits a gun interface even though its post does not launch the coordinated shells itself. `MaltaGeography` caches data statically and appends scenery markers from JSON, so offline generators must preserve a shared projection and compatible data. Generated assets require provenance and a documented build order. These tradeoffs are derived from current dependencies; proposed remedies are labelled recommendations in the next chapter.
