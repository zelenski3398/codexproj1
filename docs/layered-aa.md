# Layered Malta airfield defence

Each of the three existing airfields has four QF 3.7-inch inspired heavy positions and two Bofors 40 mm positions. All six are manual, independently damageable, and persist through dismounting. They occupy the existing graded apron at X37, clear of the runway, parking and X78 driving lane. The original north/south positions and road access remain intact. The dive-defence mission now tracks all six local guns, failing if all are lost; its six waves, operator death, delayed ordnance drain and aircraft combat are preserved.

E mounts/exits nearby guns. Arrows aim, Space gives fine adjustment, Ctrl fires. Heavy guns require a fresh Ctrl press for each shot. W/S changes heavy fuze time (0.15–8 seconds); the HUD shows seconds, reload, health and remaining shells. Bofors fire while held; the HUD shows reserve and four-round clip status. Dismounting pauses neither battle nor reload. Seek cover before bomb impact. No unoccupied gun aims or fires.

Configurations are `weapon_definitions/heavy_aa.tres` and `bofors_aa.tres`, using `scripts/aa_weapon_definition.gd`. Heavy defaults: 790 m/s, 5-second reload, 18°/s traverse, 14°/s elevation, 0.5 m recoil, 80 shells. Bofors: 850 m/s, 0.5-second cycle, four-round clips, an extra 0.5-second clip feeding delay, 45°/s traverse, 30°/s elevation, 0.12 m recoil, 240 rounds. Both have configurable 0.35° dispersion. No altitude restriction or aim assistance.

The existing pooled projectile system gains opt-in gravity and an optional AA definition. Aircraft ammunition keeps its original behaviour. Both AA pools have 96 slots and 9.81 m/s² drop, continuous swept collision and actual hitbox selection. Heavy timed rounds produce large bounded flak effects; Bofors expire silently on misses and produce compact effects only on impact. Collision with terrain/sea produces existing dust/splash effects.

Heavy direct hits apply 200 hull HP. Its blast curve has configurable knots: 150 at 0 m, 100 at 3 m, 50 at 8 m, 15 at 14 m, 0 at 15 m, interpolated between knots. Nearest component surface distance and cover rays are reused. The direct victim is excluded from blast damage. Bofors primary hull damage is 40 fuselage/tail, 50 wing, 80 engine, 60 fuel and 100 cockpit. These amounts are converted once into the existing component framework, avoiding stacked hull multipliers. Existing fuel leak/fire probability and mechanical failures remain in effect. There is no extra unavoidable damage or guaranteed fire roll.

The supplied emplacement, sandbags and crates remain the Bofors visual. Heavy positions use that emplacement foundation with an original enlarged cradle, thicker extended barrel and animated breech/recoil. This is a recognisable gameplay placeholder, not a museum-quality QF model or historical crew/reloading animation. No new terrain craters or aircraft breakup meshes are introduced: existing impact smoke, flashes and aircraft destruction are reused. Destroyed Stukas stop their AI and cannot release a subsequent bomb; already-released bombs still fall normally.

Audio is positional, attenuated and reduced in volume, with bounded voices. Bofors reuse the original procedural cannon report; heavy artillery uses a longer, lower report generated by `tools/build_heavy_aa_sound.py`. Both are clearly differentiated placeholders, **not authentic recordings**. Authentic QF and Bofors recordings should replace these assets later. The common gunner sight is retained, with weapon-specific HUD status.

## Test procedure

Open Missions → Under Dive-Bomber Attack. Walk to each labelled gun and use E to mount/dismount; check independent HP and reserve. Hold Ctrl on heavy artillery for more than five seconds: only one shot should fire. Release and press again after reload. Adjust W/S and observe timed bursts and ballistic drop. Hold Ctrl on Bofors: expect four shots at half-second intervals, a feed pause, then the next clip. Misses should not create heavy aerial clouds. Compare low Stuka passes and medium-altitude approaches; lead manually. Shoot engine, wing and fuselage surfaces, checking F3 component diagnostics. Dismount and seek cover when a Stuka dives. Bombs can independently kill operator and gun. Switch to a surviving gun; retry restores all positions, ammunition and health without duplicates.

Automated checks and remaining validation are recorded in the implementation delivery. Full six-wave player balance, subjective audio mix and consumer hardware performance still require interactive testing.
