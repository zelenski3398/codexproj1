# Bf 109 E-4 Emil and F-4 Friedrich

Choose either German fighter under **Malta · Airfield / Mode Options**. The default airborne start has the engine running, gear up and flaps neutral. For a parked start, turn off **Airborne start**, board with **E**, and start the engine with **I**.

- Hold **Q** to extend the flaps. Release to hold the angle or hold **Shift+Q** to retract.
- Use **G** for gear and **F** for the independent machine-gun and cannon batteries. Neither fighter has dedicated airbrakes.
- Use **L/J** to open or close the Emil’s radiator. The Friedrich regulates its cooling outlets automatically. Both have automatic leading-edge slats.
- Use **P** to spawn parked or airborne aircraft and transfer control between serviceable aircraft in flight. **C** adds a random AI opponent. Existing aircraft retain their own fuel, ammunition and damage until **R** restarts the session.

The Emil has a short spinner, externally braced tail, fixed tailwheel and two MG FF/M wing cannons besides its cowling machine guns. The Friedrich has a rounded spinner and wing tips, an unbraced tail, a semi-retracting tailwheel and one MG 151/20 hub cannon. The models use generic period-inspired markings. The Emil handbook gives a 40° pilot setting and a 42° rigging figure. The game uses the 42° physical stop. The Friedrich uses a 40° stop. Flap speeds, thermal response, propeller pitch, weapons and aerodynamic coefficients are gameplay approximations.
