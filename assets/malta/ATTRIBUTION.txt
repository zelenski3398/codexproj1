Malta terrain supplied by the user, imported at its original 1 metre scale.
Elevation: Planning Authority, Digital Terrain Model 2012, CC BY 4.0.
https://portal.data.gov.mt/dataset/digital-terrain-model-2012
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Coastline: © 2026 OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Source datasets, metadata and terrain generator are provided in the repository:
https://github.com/zelenski3398/codexproj1/tree/main/assets/malta/source
Changes by this game: north-up navigation chart; imported mesh LOD/collision;
approximate historical airfield markers and terrain-aligned runway/apron surfaces.
The original supplied GLB is retained unchanged. Runtime terrain elevations are
locally graded for base grounds and roads; coastline XZ, axes and metre scale are
preserved. Roads are an approximate interpretation of the user-supplied historic
map, with original generated materials; see docs/malta-road-network.md for limits.
The elevation is a coarse 2012 measured-terrain foundation, not surveyed WWII terrain.

Landscape additions: original algorithmically generated soil/limestone/scrub
detail textures, illustrative agricultural parcel patterns and decorative silhouettes.
Original surface_detail.png and cliff_detail.png artwork: CC0 1.0.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Landcover/coastal masks derive land coverage from the supplied terrain and retain
the above DTM/coastline attribution and licenses. The complete derivation script
and source data are included in the repository. These are not 1940 cadastral data,
photographic textures or a verified reconstruction of Valletta's building layout.

Cottonera urban geometry: derived from user-supplied Overpass Turbo GeoJSON,
OpenStreetMap contributors, source timestamp 2026-10-16T17:43:36Z, ODbL 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Normalized source database, selection script and derivation are included in the
repository under assets/malta/cottonera and tools/prepare_supplied_cottonera.py.
Building/landmark details are original schematic interpretations; modern OSM
footprints do not establish a verified 1942 reconstruction.
