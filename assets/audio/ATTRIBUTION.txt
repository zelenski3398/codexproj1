Runtime Ogg Vorbis copies were derived from the supplied WAV files. Engine
and gun recordings are mono for spatial playback and crossfaded at their loop
boundaries. Gun recordings are trimmed to their active firing section. Music
retains stereo and its complete duration, with a 2.2x gain below clipping.
Original-file SHA-256 hashes and exact conversion settings: conversion.json.
Reproduce conversions with tools/prepare_audio.py and the original uploads.

No Ju 87 / Jumo or MG 15 recording was supplied. The Stuka uses the supplied
Spitfire inline-engine and machine-gun sounds as temporary audio placeholders.
